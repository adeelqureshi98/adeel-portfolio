Built slowly.
Loved daily.

How It Works

From first sketch to your doorstep.

A calm, guided process — you approve every step before we build.

  1. 01

    Consult

    Tell us your room size, style and budget. Our design team visits or video-calls — free, no obligation.

  2. 02

    Design

    You get fabric swatches and a 3D preview of your exact piece. We revise until you love it.

  3. 03

    Craft

    Your piece enters the workshop — hardwood frame, foam, upholstery, polish — with photo updates along the way.

  4. 04

    Deliver

    White-glove delivery, placement in your room, and packaging taken away. You just sit down.
