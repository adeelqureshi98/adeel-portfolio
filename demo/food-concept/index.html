Our Story

It started with
one wok.

Zaiqa House began as a home kitchen with a single karahi and a stubborn belief: desi food tastes best when it's cooked the slow way — fresh masalas, open flame, and patience.

Word travelled faster than our delivery bikes. The weekend karahi nights became a queue, the queue became a kitchen, and the kitchen became the Zaiqa House you see today — but the recipe hasn't changed. We still grind our masalas every morning, still seal every biryani on dum, still slap every naan to order.

Some call it old-fashioned. We call it the whole point.

Why Zaiqa House

Three promises,
kept daily.

Hygiene, audited

Sealed, tamper-proof packaging on every order. Our kitchen runs daily deep-cleans and weekly third-party hygiene audits — because trust is an ingredient too.

Taste, uncompromised

Masalas ground fresh every single morning. Desi ghee, never vanaspati. Meat sourced daily from trusted suppliers — if it isn't fresh, it doesn't get cooked.

Delivery, hot & fast

Insulated boxes, 45-minute average delivery, and food that arrives steaming — or the delivery is on us. That's the Zaiqa guarantee.
